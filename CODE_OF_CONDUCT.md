# Code of Conduct

LOKI KEYS wants this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time, privacy and different points of view.
- Own your mistakes and help fix them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Anything that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Email **support@lokikeys.com** with the subject "Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially and promptly. The maintainers may edit or remove content, or block people who break these rules, temporarily or permanently.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
