Delivery and Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: if your key won't activate and we can't fix it, you get a working replacement or your money back.

Delivery

All products are digital licence keys delivered by email to the address you give at checkout, once payment is confirmed. There is no physical shipping and no shipping charge. Orders held for a payment or fraud review can take longer; we will email you if that happens.

If your key does not activate

  1. Email support@lokikeys.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a screenshot of the error.
  2. We will check the edition, region and device with you. Most problems are solved at this step.
  3. If the key is faulty, we send a working replacement or, if you prefer or we cannot replace it, a full refund.

When we can't offer a refund

  • The key has already been activated successfully. A licence key can't be returned once it is used, much like a gift card.
  • The key was bought for the wrong region, edition or device after the product page stated it clearly, unless it has not been revealed or redeemed.
  • A region restriction was bypassed with a VPN or false account details.

If you ordered by mistake and have not opened or redeemed the key, contact us within 14 days and we will cancel the order and refund you.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days after we approve them, depending on your bank. If you used a discount code, you are refunded the amount you actually paid.

Your statutory rights

This policy does not affect your rights under consumer protection law.
