Delivery and Refund Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version: unopened keys can be refunded within 14 days, and if a key doesn't work you get a working replacement or your money back.

Delivery

Every product is a digital license key. It is emailed to the address you give at checkout within 24 hours of payment, usually much sooner, together with step-by-step activation instructions. Nothing physical is shipped and there is no shipping charge. If an order is held for a payment or fraud check, we email you straight away and it may take longer.

Each product page shows, before you buy: the number of devices, the license period, compatible systems and the activation region (for example "USA / Canada" or "Global").

How to ask

  1. Email support@lokikeys.com with your order number. For a key that doesn't work, add a screenshot of the error message.
  2. We reply within 48 hours (Monday to Saturday) and check the edition, region and device with you.
  3. We send the replacement or confirm your refund by email.

A refund can be refused only if a region restriction was bypassed (for example with a VPN or false account details) or the order was fraudulent.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days after we approve them, depending on your bank. If you used a discount code, you are refunded the amount you actually paid.
