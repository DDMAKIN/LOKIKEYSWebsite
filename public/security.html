Security & Trust

Last reviewed: 21 November 2026

This page explains exactly how LOKI KEYS keeps your account, your payment and your data safe, so you don't have to take it on trust.

The short version: we never see your card details or your password, the site loads no tracking without your permission, and every change to our code is checked automatically before it goes live.

Payments

  • You pay on Wix's hosted checkout, which is PCI DSS compliant. Your card number goes straight to Wix and its payment providers; it never touches our website or our team.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars before you pay, with any sales tax shown at checkout and no added card fees.

Your account

  • Accounts run on the Wix store's member system. Passwords are stored and checked by Wix; we never receive them.
  • Continue with Google is handled by Wix and Google. We only receive your name and email.
  • Sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 PKCE standard: each sign-in link works once, only in the browser tab that started it, and expires after 15 minutes.
  • Sign-in forms don't reveal whether an email has an account, repeated wrong passwords are slowed down, and Wix blocks known abusive networks.
  • Where mobile sign-in is offered, the one-time SMS code is sent and checked by Google Firebase with bot protection, and each code can be used only once.

Privacy by default

  • No analytics or ad tracking loads unless you agree. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, advertising cookies stay off automatically.
  • Fonts and images are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn't share your IP address with font providers.
  • We never sell your personal data. See our privacy policy for your rights in India, the US and Canada.

How the website is protected

  • HTTPS everywhere with HSTS, and a strict Content Security Policy that blocks scripts from anywhere we haven't approved.
  • The site can't be framed by other websites (clickjacking protection) and sends strict referrer, permissions and cross-origin policies.
  • Every code change runs automated checks: type-checking, security tests for sign-in, a build, a scan for known vulnerable packages, GitHub CodeQL code scanning and a scan for accidentally committed secrets.
  • Dependencies and build tools are pinned to exact versions and updated through reviewed pull requests.

Report a security issue

If you think you've found a vulnerability, email support@lokikeys.com with the subject "Security". Please include the page, what you found and how to reproduce it.

  • We acknowledge reports within 3 working days and keep you updated until it's fixed.
  • Please don't access other people's data, run denial-of-service or spam tests, or use social engineering. Testing done in good faith under these rules won't lead to legal action from us.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix an issue before telling anyone else.

Our machine-readable contact is at /.well-known/security.txt.

Thanks

We'll gladly credit researchers who report issues responsibly here, with their permission.
