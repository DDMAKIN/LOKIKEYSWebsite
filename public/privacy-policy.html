Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how LOKI KEYS ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this website, buy from us or contact us. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Who we are

LOKI KEYS, an independent online seller of software licence keys based in India.
Email: support@lokikeys.com

2. What data we collect

  • Order data you give us at checkout: name, email address, billing country and payment details. Payment details are entered on Wix's hosted checkout and handled by Wix and its payment providers; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages you send us: your email address and anything you include, such as an order number or a screenshot.
  • Technical data: your browser and device type and IP address, which our hosting provider and font provider (Google Fonts) receive when the page loads.
  • Account data, if you create an account: your first name, email address, mobile number, password and whether you want marketing emails. Passwords are stored and checked by Wix; we never see them. If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with our store. If you sign in with your mobile number (where offered), the one-time SMS code is sent and checked by Google Firebase, and we store the verified number on your account.
  • Shopping and sign-in data in your browser: your bag and a session token are kept in your browser's local storage so your cart and sign-in survive a page reload. They are not used to track you across other sites. Signing out removes the sign-in token.

3. Advertising and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. If we add tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads or the Meta Pixel, we will list them here, update the date above and ask for your consent where the law requires it before they run.

4. Why we use your data

  • To process your order and email you your licence key
  • To help with activation, replacements and refunds
  • To prevent fraud and meet our legal and tax obligations
  • To send you marketing, only if you have agreed to receive it

We process order data to perform our contract with you, and other data with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing us.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that run the store: Wix (store, customer accounts, checkout and order management), its payment processors, Google (sign-in with Google, and SMS codes through Firebase if you sign in with your mobile number), our website host, and our email provider. We share data with authorities only where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and other data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for. Then we delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, delete your account, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Email support@lokikeys.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Grievances

For complaints about how we handle your data, write to our grievance contact at support@lokikeys.com with the subject "Data grievance". If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

This website uses HTTPS encryption and security headers, sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 PKCE standard so sign-in codes can't be reused by anyone else, and payments run on Wix's PCI-compliant hosted checkout. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
